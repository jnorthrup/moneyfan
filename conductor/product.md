# Product Guide

## Product Name
moneyfan

## Product Summary
moneyfan is a local-first HRM trading research system focused on rapid iteration, reproducible training, and safe promotion of models into runtime execution.

## Primary User
Solo quant operator who trains locally, validates rigorously, and wants controllable deployment in paper/live-preview modes.

## Core Capabilities
- HRM model training and checkpointing.
- Regime-aware evaluation and promotion gates.
- Runtime execution with auditable controls.
- Operator-facing telemetry and artifacts.

## Constraints
- Local compute is the primary resource.
- Brownfield codebase with ongoing experimentation.
- Training speed and reproducibility matter more than theoretical completeness.
